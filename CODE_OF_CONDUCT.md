# Code of Conduct

## The short version

Be respectful, be honest, and argue about the work, not the person.

## Expected

- Disagree about code, design, and correctness claims openly and directly —
  this project treats a failing test or a disproven claim as useful
  information, not an attack.
- Back claims with evidence (a differential case, a measurement, a
  reproduction) rather than assertion, and say plainly when something is
  unmeasured or uncertain rather than implying more confidence than you have.
- Assume good faith in review and issue discussion; ask before assuming
  malice or incompetence.
- Respect maintainers' and contributors' time: search existing issues first,
  keep reports reproducible and scoped, keep discussion on-topic.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, harassment, or discriminatory language or behavior
  directed at any person, in this repository or in any project space
  (issues, pull requests, discussions).
- Deliberately misrepresenting test results, benchmarks, or the state of the
  code — this project's entire premise is that claims are checked against
  reality; misrepresenting them undermines the thing the project is for.
- Sustained disruption of discussion, or repeatedly re-litigating a settled
  decision without new evidence.

## Scope

This applies to all project spaces — the repository, its issues and pull
requests, and any other forum explicitly designated as a project space —
and to anyone representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Violations may be reported by opening an issue tagged for maintainer
attention, or, if the report itself needs to stay private, by contacting a
maintainer directly. Maintainers will review reports and may edit, remove, or
reject contributions and comments, and may temporarily or permanently ban
anyone whose behavior is deemed inappropriate, threatening, or harmful.
Maintainers commit to applying this consistently, and to explaining
enforcement decisions when asked in good faith.
